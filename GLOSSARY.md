# Together

A shared household app for one couple: shopping, recipes, plans and the small things they look after together.

## Shopping and offers

**Product**:
A thing the couple buys, independent of any store, price or week (e.g. "Vollmilch 1 l").
_Avoid_: Item (an item is one entry on the shopping list), article

**Shopping item**:
One open or bought entry on the shared shopping list.
_Avoid_: Product, task

**Favorite**:
A Product the couple saved so they can add it again quickly; it has no store and no price.
_Avoid_: Saved offer, bookmark

**Store**:
One of the six supermarket chains in Düren the app shows Offers for: Kaufland, Lidl, ALDI SÜD, REWE, Netto, EDEKA.
_Avoid_: Shop, market, retailer

**Brochure**:
A store's published weekly list of offers for a fixed period.
_Avoid_: Prospekt (German UI word only), flyer, catalogue

**Offer**:
A time-limited price for a Product at one Store, taken from a Brochure, valid from one date to another.
_Avoid_: Deal, discount, sale, Angebot (German UI word only)

**Brochure screenshot**:
A picture of a Brochure page that a household member shares with the app so its Offers can be checked and saved for that household.
_Avoid_: Scan, upload, photo import

**Regular price**:
The non-offer price, known only when the Brochure states it; never estimated.
_Avoid_: Old price, RRP

**Offer week**:
The calendar week (Monday–Sunday) whose Offers the app shows as current.
_Avoid_: Brochure week

**Favorite match**:
An Offer that looks like a Favorite, either *sure* or *maybe*; the couple can reject a maybe so it does not come back.
_Avoid_: Hit, alert

**Ingredient match**:
An Offer that covers an ingredient of a saved recipe.
_Avoid_: Recipe offer, Zutatentreffer (German UI word only)

**Stock-up hint**:
A suggestion to buy several of a long-lasting Product that the couple buys often and that is on Offer now.
_Avoid_: Vorrat tip, bulk deal

**Price estimate**:
The app's rough built-in supermarket price for a recipe ingredient; not an Offer and never shown as a saving.
_Avoid_: Real price, live price
